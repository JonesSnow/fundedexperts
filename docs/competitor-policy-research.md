# Competitor Policy Research — Reference Only

**Accessed:** 2026-10-13
**Purpose:** Review official public policy pages from FundedNext and FundingPips for product-disclosure patterns and policy questions. This material is research context only. It is not Funded Experts policy, pricing, approval, or implementation guidance by itself.

## Findings and classification

All items below are **Competitor reference**. No competitor value or prohibition has been copied into the Funded Experts UI or rule configuration.

| Topic | FundedNext official pages reviewed | FundingPips official pages reviewed | Product-design observation for Funded Experts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evaluation structures and targets | Stellar 1-Step describes one evaluation phase with 10% target; Stellar 2-Step describes phase-specific targets of 8% and 5%. The help center separates product families and account phases. | Model comparison distinguishes 1-Step Flex, multiple 2-Step models, and Zero instant Master; target values differ across those models. | A headline such as “one step” or “two step” is insufficient. Publish target and stage conditions by exact product and version. |
| Loss and drawdown | Stellar 1-Step help lists a 3% daily limit and 6% maximum limit; Stellar 2-Step lists a 5% daily and 10% maximum limit. Separate help articles explain calculation and server-time behavior. | The model comparison shows different daily limits and static limits across evaluation models, while Zero uses a trailing model; its detailed page explains baseline and reset conditions. | A percentage must be paired with its baseline, reference point, timezone, open-P&L/fee treatment, and whether it is static or trailing. These competitor calculations are not Funded Experts calculations. |
| Activity and progress | Stellar 1-Step specifies two separate calendar trading days; Stellar 2-Step specifies five per phase. Both pages describe how target completion interacts with minimum activity. | Published model comparisons and individual model pages vary by phase and account model; Zero describes profitable-day conditions for rewards. | Minimum trade count, trading day, profitable day and payout day are distinct concepts and should not be conflated. |
| News | FundedNext's news page allows news trading in challenge stages and describes a separate reward-share treatment for some funded-account states and a defined event window. | FundingPips Zero describes a restricted high-impact news window with breach consequences; this differs from other product models. | State the event source, time window, instruments and stage applicability for each Funded Experts product if approved. |
| Overnight/weekend | FundedNext's help page says overnight and weekend holding is generally allowed across the referenced account families, subject to swap charges. | FundingPips Zero explicitly disallows weekend holding; other FundingPips models may have different terms or add-ons. | Do not use a universal holding statement across account categories without product-specific configuration. |
| Strategies, copy and automation | FundedNext's restricted-strategy page lists multiple exploitative practices and account/device sharing; its permitted tools and exceptions are product-specific. | FundingPips' conduct page describes prohibited third-party trading and inbound copy policies, while allowing some own-account or EA use under conditions. | Define allowed, restricted and prohibited behaviors and exceptions explicitly; an undefined strategy must not be labeled prohibited by borrowing competitor language. |
| Direct / no-evaluation models | FundedNext pages describe product-specific account progression and KYC steps after passing their evaluation. | FundingPips Zero is described as a no-evaluation model with its own trailing loss, news, weekend, activity, reset and reward conditions. | “Direct funded” does not mean no limits, instant credentials, live capital, or immediate payout. Define its own eligibility and operating conditions. |
| Reset/retry and rewards | FundedNext challenge pages include product-specific reset, reward-share and payout options. Some terms vary by purchase/reset date and add-on. | FundingPips model pages document reset eligibility, timing, discounts, reward schedules and consistency requirements with model-specific differences. | Reset price, retries, payout splits, frequency and timing are commercial terms requiring explicit owner approval and supported workflow. |
| Identity and review | FundedNext's Stellar 1-Step page describes KYC after passing the challenge and specific document steps. | FundingPips conduct/security pages describe trader identity/account ownership checks and possible review/enforcement. | Email verification and KYC are separate. Funded Experts must define eligibility, data collection, timing, appeals and retention before making promises. |

## Limits

The sources are official help-center/legal pages, but policies can change and may depend on product, purchase date, region, add-ons, or account state. This research summarizes published examples as accessed on the date above; it is not legal advice or a current exhaustive product quote. No competitor account was created and no payment or trading action was attempted.
